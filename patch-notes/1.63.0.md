# v1.63.0: Commander and Warrior

**Commander:** the deck builder prices each command per ⚡, suggests a deck for your doctrine and keeps four saved decks. In a run: cooldown seconds on every command, ⚡ per second, a Perks panel with stacks, a tip on each perk card, a gold-paid perk reroll and a once-a-run quest swap. New quests (win a wave without a command; five hits on a marked foe) and perks (Spotter, Reserve Fund). The summary shows commands used with ⚡ spent, reload and headshot rates and your best combo.
**Warrior:** a camp bar for standing still, the Second Wind and Hunter perks, and a build line on the summary.
**Settings:** Large controls (44px buttons in a run).
**Accessibility:** command and fire button names now match their visible text.
**Unchanged on purpose:** ranked boards, scoring, server rules. Commander and Warrior are local modes.

See IMPROVEMENTS.md for the list.
