# v1.67.0: Dominion content

**Scenarios:** First Steps (a guided six-turn tutorial), Island Realm, Crowded Continent, Last Kingdom.
**New:** four buildings, two techs, three policies, three Wonders, ten events.
**Compatibility:** only campaigns started from now on get the new content; a war in progress keeps its old rules and still loads.
**Unchanged on purpose:** ranked boards, Dominion battles and scoring.
