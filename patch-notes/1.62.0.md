# v1.62.0: weekly modes in more detail

**Sprint:** split time for every wave, "ahead of / behind your best" on the wave banner, slowest leg and percent-of-minimum on the summary, a suggested level, and your best splits kept per level.
**Gauntlet:** stage times on the summary, the next map named on the boss banner, newly opened towers listed, and a hard-ground warning one stage ahead.
**Endurance and Last Stand:** kill-pace chart, "where it broke" (first tower lost, first foe through), foes-to-next-repair on the clock, longest hold per map, a confirm before Begin with plots empty, and the strength ladder on the Last Stand prep screen.
**Warfront:** favoured foes by name, the past six warlords with your best wave against each, favoured-foe breaches on the summary, an orange Practice tag off the weekly map.
**All weekly modes:** time left in the week on the prep screen; Retry this week on the summary.
**Fixed along the way:** the Endurance wave label stayed on "Ready" for the first twelve seconds after Begin.
**Unchanged on purpose:** balance numbers, boards, scoring and server validation.
