# v1.65.0: Dominion and Sea Siege

**Dominion:** an end-turn warning (Settings toggle), treaty-expiry and revolt reminders from the advisor, the next six seasons on the season chip, a policy income preview, a battle brief, and a score breakdown with campaign stats at the end of a war.
**Sea Siege:** a fleet forecast in the wave strip, hull multipliers on tower buttons, and a sea line on the summary.
**Unchanged on purpose:** ranked boards, scoring, save formats, server rules.
