# 1.69.2 · A commander that keeps building

- **Fix:** in Commander and Warrior runs the engineers stopped upgrading once every plot held a tower (they kept waiting to build one more), so towers froze around level 5. They now put the gold into the towers they have: every tier, the specialization, and then mastery stars for as long as the run lasts.
- **Smarter specializations:** each tower's path is chosen from the numbers in the specialization against the waves coming (armor, bosses, swarms, flyers, magic-proof foes, runners), not always the first one listed.
- **Planning ahead:** the engineers read the next three waves together, so they keep anti-air up between two air waves and do not swap on one wave's whim.
- **Swapping towers (Commander):** once the plots are full, a tower that the coming waves have outgrown can be sold and replaced on the same plot with the type they call for, at most once per break. A tower gets six waves to prove itself, and heavily built towers need a much clearer reason to go.
